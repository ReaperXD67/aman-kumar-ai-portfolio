## Practical limits

The shared opening caps its base rendering area at 1.8 million pixels, uses demand frames during the scroll route, and suspends offscreen/hidden rendering. This is a resource policy, not a measured frame-rate guarantee. Vite still reports large shared Three/animation chunks.
