## Research basis

The configuration follows LinkedIn's own product behavior and avoids unsupported ranking claims:

- [Headline help](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a542926/editing-your-headline?lang=en): the headline appears in search and can express expertise separately from the current-position title.
- [Recruiter features](https://business.linkedin.com/hire/recruiter/recruiter-features): recruiters can filter by job titles, skills, and locations and use keyword/Boolean search. Accurate standardized skills and exact employment titles are therefore useful inputs; this does not establish a fixed ranking formula.
- [Featured FAQ](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a552452/featured-section-on-your-profile-faqs?lang=en): external links and media show proof to profile visitors; Featured itself is not search-discoverable. Existing external-link cards were verified available in this account.
- [Practices to avoid](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a526134): profile completeness and accuracy matter; repetitive keywords and inflated employment/education are discouraged.
- [Portrait guidance](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog/product-tips/tips-for-taking-professional-linkedin-profile-pictures): use a recent recognizable photograph, a clear crop, and an unobtrusive background. No inference about competence is made from facial appearance.
- [Search Appearances](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a553050/view-your-profile-search-appearances): evaluate the kinds of searches and engagement visible in the product after changes. No search or shortlist guarantee is made.
