# Aman Portfolio Design System

## Direction

Cinematic intelligence interface: near-black canvas, white/graphite typography, editorial scale, hairline borders and original procedural geometry. One opening renderer and camera carry the fluid Decision Loom into the curved project world. Actual architecture and measured artifact frames continue that visual lineage through Method, the natural portrait, professional evidence and contact. The MotionSites screenshot is inspiration for atmosphere and contrast only; no brand, layout, or copy is duplicated. Runway-inspired principles strengthen the current system: the visual artifact leads, interface chrome recedes, imagery is cinematic, and depth comes from light and composition rather than decorative shadows.
