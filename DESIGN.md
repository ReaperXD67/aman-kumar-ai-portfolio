# Aman Portfolio Design System

## Direction

Cinematic intelligence interface: near-black canvas, white/graphite typography, dense monospace telemetry, editorial scale, hairline borders, and one live procedural Three.js object. The MotionSites screenshot is inspiration for atmosphere and contrast only; no brand, layout, or copy is duplicated. Runway-inspired principles strengthen the current system: the visual artifact leads, interface chrome recedes, imagery is cinematic, and depth comes from light and composition rather than decorative shadows.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#050505`; raised surfaces: `#0b0b0b` and `#111212`.
- Text: `#f5f5f1`; muted: `#8b8f91`; hairlines: `#252728`.
- Primary signal: vermilion `#ff5f38`; proof/success: acid `#d8ff4f`; secondary signal: blue `#7ea8ff`.
- Display/body: Cabinet Grotesk with Space Grotesk Variable as the fallback, regular-to-medium weights, tight tracking no tighter than `-0.04em`.
- Technical labels: JetBrains Mono Variable, uppercase, wide tracking.
- Shape: square functional controls and image frames; only status lights are circular.
- Elevation: borders and surface contrast, never drop shadows on content cards.

A 144-ribbon GPU field and thousands of signals morph through sensor array, reasoning braid, and execution vector. Its directional woven geometry must never resemble the identity kernel's orbital reactor. On phones the sculpture occupies its own clear upper stage above the statement and actions.

- Selected work is the Engineering X-ray: a real-capture filmstrip opens an editorial project title and interface or explicitly labeled blueprint above four spatial architecture layers. Switching projects passes the previous capture through eight alternating optical shutters over the already-rendered next instrument. Phones use a native horizontal snap rail. Surface, Structure, and Decisions stages plus a native inspection-depth slider expose failure, engineering choice, honest boundary, and direct source. KarixMC leads with its real product capture and game-to-web trust boundary.
- Method is a black Validation Chamber embedded in the light editorial surface. Its live canvas convergence field changes with four semantic stages while a dedicated readout explains the engineering law and trace.
- Identity is a two-view portrait instrument. Person reveals Aman's approved real photograph through a finite raster assembly, then holds a clear portrait with an editorial nameplate. System exposes the existing WebGL Systems Kernel. A compact portrait signature in the hero links directly to this frame. This supersedes the earlier no-portrait direction at Aman's request on 2026-09-05.
- Experience is a horizontal three-signal career selector feeding one large operating-evidence stage, followed by a separate education signal. It must not reuse the selected-work console’s left-index anatomy.
- Résumé and micro1 certification live in a two-artifact proof vault. Both open their original source in a focused native dialog, and the résumé reads its URL/version from `/profile/resume.json` at runtime.
- Contact is a light editorial split with a real validated form that hands a pre-filled brief directly to Aman’s email client, plus visible email and phone fallbacks.
- Mobile collapses to one deliberate column with 44px minimum touch targets and no horizontal page overflow.

## Motion

- Entrance and hover motion uses short directional movement with exponential easing.
- The hero opens with one Observe → Reason → Execute → Reason performance, then keeps a low-amplitude signal flow. Pointer movement bends the field; direct clicks or semantic mode buttons take over immediately. Pause stops the field. Reduced motion shows a settled composition and direct state changes. GPU rendering is bounded to approximately 2.2 million base pixels, with 6,800 signals on desktop and 2,800 on narrow screens; no per-frame vertex-array or React-state updates.
- The primary authored moment is the scroll-controlled cognitive descent between proof and work: the hero core fractures, the camera passes through signal/reason/action gates and real project artifacts, then the sequence releases into evidence inspection.
- The Engineering X-ray is the analytical counterpoint to that descent. A short first-scroll peel reveals depth; any button, slider or keyboard interaction immediately takes control. Spatial layers transform with CSS, without an additional WebGL context or pinned scroll. Source-linked decisions remain readable and functional with reduced motion.
- Each X-ray layer has a source-grounded routed schematic rather than a repeated three-label grid. A flat active schematic keeps branching and refusal paths readable beside the notes. Layer/trace selection may play one finite route sweep; dragging depth must not continuously restart it. AtlasLM's retrieval fusion, the agent's ownership boundary and KarixMC's signed delivery path have distinct topology. These are illustrated design relationships, never live traffic or executed test results.
- Its failure walkthrough adds one finite sequence per project: expected input → broken assumption → implemented guard. Explicit playback synchronizes the surface, structure and decision depths. Manual steps remain available; reduced motion jumps directly to the guard. The narration links to real source and labels illustrative inputs clearly.
- The identity kernel boots in four authored acts: signal corruption, voxel convergence, topology mapping, and stack compilation. The assembled system responds magnetically to the pointer and exposes a user-triggered overclock state. Career and artifact changes remain subordinate.
- WebGL scenes mount only when near the viewport so the hero and descent do not compete for GPU resources.
- Continuous ticker and WebGL rendering pause or become static under reduced-motion/offscreen conditions.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables continuous movement, bloom, and decorative transitions while preserving the final readable state.
